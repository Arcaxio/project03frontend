# Project 03 - Maimai B50 Maker
Very niche idea I will admit, but hey, no one really made one yet, so I decided to take matters into my own hands! Huge thanks to zetaraku for the endpoints, most of which isn't possible without their contributions.

Most of this is written with Jules, and I gotta say it's pretty neat that Jules can do this pretty well.

Yeah don't worry, no data is stolen or whatever, everything is stored locally, so if you clear your cache and what not, your b50 board will get wiped, so be warned.

## What it can do
* Create a b50 from scratch by searching up charts you're trying to target
* Check charts that you have obtained the score for
> Checked charts will look darker compared to unchecked charts 
* Run offline granted you have accessed it online before
> Chart data is stored locally, however images will not load if they haven't been loaded in before

## What it can't do
* Import your existing B50 from maimai.net
> I have not figured out how to extract the data from maimai.net
* Cross-sync to other devices, the data stays on your device 
> You can however, export your b50, and reimport it on a separate device. May consider adding this functionality in the future
